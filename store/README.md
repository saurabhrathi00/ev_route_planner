## Full description (max 4000)

EVRoute plans one drive at a time and answers the question every other app
skips: how much charge will actually be left when you get there.

It reads the road. Every 100 metres of climb and descent along your route, the
air you push through at the speed you drive, the weight of everyone on board,
and the temperature and wind forecast for the hour you will be at each point.
Distance alone cannot tell you what a mountain road costs; this does.

• See the whole drive as a picture — drag the car along the route and watch
  the battery fall, or press play and let it drive itself
• Know exactly where you drop to your reserve, and what to charge to
• Find chargers along the corridor, ranked on whether they will be usable
  when you arrive rather than on raw power
• Understand what made the drive expensive: elevation, wind, traffic, climate
• Teach it your car — log real trips and the model corrects toward how you
  actually drive

Free, with no account and no sign-up. There is no server: every calculation
happens on your phone, and nothing about your drives leaves it.

Built on open data — OpenStreetMap, OpenRouteService, Open-Meteo and Open
Charge Map. Add your own Google Maps key in Settings if you want Google
routing, search and live charger availability; the app works fully without it.
